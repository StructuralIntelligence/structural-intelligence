# Structural Intelligence Canon

## Overview

The Structural Intelligence Canon is the authoritative body of doctrine, definitions, principles, books, reference works, visual models, and educational materials that collectively define the Structural Intelligence field.

The canon preserves the identity, continuity, language, architecture, and evolution of Structural Intelligence across all divisions, publications, and future expansions.

---

## Purpose

The canon exists to:

* Preserve doctrinal integrity
* Establish a permanent body of work
* Maintain consistency across all divisions
* Define the official language and visual architecture of the field
* Protect the continuity of Structural Intelligence
* Provide a reference framework for future generations

## Primary Canon Works

### 2. Ten Commandments of Structural Intelligence

### 3. Structural Intelligence Doctrine

The primary doctrinal framework of the field.

### 4. SI = No SI — Book 1

The origin-law volume establishing the foundational logic of Structural Intelligence.

### 5. SSI Tree of Origin (Tree #1)

The upstream gateway doctrine establishing where structure comes from (`Supreme Super Intelligence (SSI)`, `Existence`, `Natural Law`, `Structure`, and `Structural Formations`), where visibility ends (**Structural Horizon** and **Structural Blindness**), how mechanics hold across scale (**Scale Invariance**), and how structures enter **Observation** before operational analysis begins.

### 6. Tree of Structural Intelligence (Tree #2)

The canonical operational visual model of Structural Intelligence once a structure enters observation and analysis. The Tree maps the Root System, structural intelligence, core rings, structural drift, the Structural Intelligence Paradox, preservation, transformation, and extraction pathways, observable outcomes, and the Full Circle Principle.

### 7. Atlas Dictionary of Structural Intelligence

The official lexicon and language foundation of the field.

## 🌱 Tree #1: The SSI Tree of Origin & 🌳 Tree #2: The Tree of Structural Intelligence

**Tree #1: The SSI Tree of Origin** (*Origin → Visibility → Observation*) establishes where structure comes from, where visibility ends (**Structural Horizon** and **Structural Blindness**), how mechanics hold across scale (**Scale Invariance**), and how a structure enters **Observation** before analysis begins.

🌱 [SSI Tree of Origin (Tree #1)](tree-of-origin.md)

**Tree #2: The Tree of Structural Intelligence** (*Understanding → Action → Outcome → Continuation*) serves as the canonical operational visual map once a structure enters observation and analysis. While the Atlas Dictionary defines the language of Structural Intelligence, the Tree illustrates how foundational structural principles develop into analysis, record drift through time, diverge across preservation, transformation, or extraction pathways, and ultimately produce the outcomes that determine survival and evolution.

The Tree functions as the visual index of the Atlas Dictionary and the operational map of Structural Intelligence doctrine.
