## How to Read the Tree (Step-by-Step)

Read the Debate Tree from **top to bottom** across the three upper branches, down through each branch's **routing banner**, and into the **Core Definition (Think Tank Winner)** at the center trunk:

### 1. Narrow Domain Angles → Reclassified as Sub-Layers
* **What You See:** *Engineering / BIM Only*, *Digital Twin / Telemetry*, and *AI / Control Architecture*.
* **How to Read It:** These are real, high-value applications of structure, not the full definition of Structural Intelligence itself. Treating any single domain or software category as the whole definition narrows the concept too far.
* **Routing Rule:** Reclassified as **Sub-Layers**—kept as concrete domain examples, diagnostic toolkits, or applied divisions rather than replacing the universal definition.

### 2. Over-Broad / Meta Claims → Separated into Doctrine
* **What You See:** *Architecture of Everything*, *Unanchored Metaphysics*, and *Single-Verb Bias*.
* **How to Read It:** Sweeping catchphrases or unanchored meta-claims can sound grand while leaving the operational mechanics vague, and focusing on only one action verb misses the full range of structural work.
* **Routing Rule:** Separated into **Doctrine**—housed in Origin doctrine, foundational principles, and the Structural Intelligence Trees (`Tree #1–#3` and broader Forest models) rather than standing in for a crisp core definition.

### 3. Core Capacity & Scope → Surviving Core
* **What You See:** *Variable Intelligence Level* and *Create, Design, Engineer, Reverse Engineer*, leading to *Proximity-neutral • Infinite scope beyond today's vantage points*.
* **How to Read It:** This column captures what survives filtering—the underlying capacity that holds across different levels of intelligence, balances creating, designing, engineering, and reverse engineering, applies at any proximity, and scales across infinite scope beyond today's vantage points.
* **Routing Rule:** Forms the **Surviving Core** that feeds directly into the consolidated definition.

### 4. Core Definition (Think Tank Winner)
* **How to Read It:** All three filtered pathways converge into the **Core Definition (Think Tank Winner)** at the heart of the tree. Any specialized domain, doctrine layer, or division model in the Structural Intelligence ecosystem should align with—or branch cleanly from—this core capacity.
