# Structural Intelligence Universe

## Overview

The Structural Intelligence Universe is the highest-level framework within Structural Intelligence.

It provides the organizational structure through which divisions, domains, artifacts, protocols, doctrine, dictionaries, and educational systems are connected.

## Relationship to the Tree of Structural Intelligence

The Tree of Structural Intelligence serves as the operational map of the Structural Intelligence Universe.

While the Universe model explains how divisions, domains, artifacts, protocols, and AI engines are organized, the Tree explains how structures themselves emerge, experience pressure, drift, adaptation, pathway divergence, survival, and evolution.

Together, the Universe and the Tree provide the organizational and operational architecture of Structural Intelligence.

## Universe Model

The Structural Intelligence Universe organizes structure into nested layers:

1. Universe
2. Galaxies (Divisions)
3. Domains
4. Artifacts
5. Protocols
6. AI Engines
7. Observable Outcomes

Each layer inherits structure from the layer above it.

## Purpose

The Structural Intelligence Universe exists to:

* Connect all Structural Intelligence divisions
