## Structural Blindness

Structural Blindness occurs when individuals, organizations, or systems fail to observe or recognize underlying structures, relationships, drift mechanisms, or future states that lie beyond their current **Structural Horizon** (**Visibility Failure**—where a structure exists across scale, yet has not entered observation).

The result is confusion, repeated mistakes, instability, and preventable failure.

Structural Intelligence seeks to reveal and address those hidden structures across scale (**Scale Invariance**) once they enter view.

---

## Recognition

Recognition begins when a structure within the **Structural Horizon** crosses into active **Observation** (the threshold of **Tree #1: The SSI Tree of Origin** handing off into **Tree #2: The Tree of Structural Intelligence**).

Once visible and observed, structure can be:

* Understood
* Mapped
* Improved
* Stabilized
* Scaled
* Preserved
