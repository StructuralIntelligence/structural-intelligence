# Overview

The MED-1 Medical Readiness Core Tree is the primary doctrine tree for **Safety Manual Book 9 – Medical Response, First Aid & Onboard Health Safety Manual**.

Unlike traditional medical-response systems that begin at the point of injury or illness, MED-1 begins before the emergency occurs. The tree emphasizes early awareness, observation, communication, and disciplined action to prevent escalation.

MED-1 is part of the **Structural Intelligence Forest**, applying Structural Intelligence principles to maritime medical readiness, first aid, injury assessment, emergency response, and post-incident recovery.
